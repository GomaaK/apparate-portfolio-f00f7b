Together with Doodle Factory

Doodle Factory is a lifestyle design house that transforms underserved children’s doodles into dreams. Since 2016, they have been “empowering 15,000 underserved children” through design. We are proud to be the manufacturing partner behind many of their collaborations, from sportswear to scarves. Every product here carries a doodle, a partner and a purpose. And there is more to come.

Questions brands ask us

Frequently asked questions

What products do you manufacture?

Sportswear, knitwear, everyday and lifestyle wear, outerwear, baby and kidswear, and scarves and textile accessories such as printed scarves and tote bags, across knitted, woven and denim fabrics.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Our minimum order is 100 pieces per color. Final minimums are confirmed in a project-specific feasibility review, because materials, trims and construction can affect them.
